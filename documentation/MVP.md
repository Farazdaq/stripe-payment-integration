### 1. Executive Summary
The purpose of this project is to develop an online food delivery platform that connects customers with local restaurants and enables seamless ordering, tracking, and delivery of food items.
The system aims to improve customer convenience, reduce manual ordering processes, and increase restaurant sales by providing a centralized digital ordering platform accessible via mobile and web applications.

### 2. Background
Currently, customers rely on phone calls or direct restaurant visits to place orders, which leads to delays, miscommunication, and limited ordering visibility.
Restaurants also lack a unified digital system to manage incoming orders efficiently.
This project aims to digitize the food ordering process and improve operational efficiency for both customers and restaurant owners.

### 4. Problem Statement
- Increase online food orders by 50% within 6 months
- Reduce average order processing time from 10 minutes to 3 minutes
- Achieve 95% customer satisfaction rate
- Reduce order cancellation rate by 30%
- Ensure system uptime of 99.9%

### 5. Target Users / Stakeholders

|-------------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Customers         | End Users         | Browse restaurants and place orders      |
| Restaurants       | Service Providers | Manage menus and orders                  |
| Delivery Drivers  | Logistics         | Deliver orders to customers              |
| Admin Team        | System Operators  | Manage platform operations               |
| Business Owners   | Decision Makers   | Oversee performance and strategy         |

### 6. Project Scope (In / Out)
  ##### In Scope
  - User registration and authentication
  - Restaurant listing and menu browsing
  - Food ordering system
  - Payment integration
  - Real-time order tracking
  - Admin dashboard
  ##### Out of Scope
  - Restaurant kitchen management systems
  - Delivery vehicle fleet management
  - Offline ordering system
  - Loyalty rewards program (future phase)

### 7. High-Level Requirements
  - Users shall be able to register and log into the system
  - Users shall be able to browse restaurants and menus
  - Users shall be able to place and cancel orders
  - System shall allow online payment processing
  - Restaurants shall be able to manage menu items
  - System shall provide real-time order tracking updates
  - Admin shall be able to manage users and restaurants

### 8. Non-Functional Requirements
  - Performance: System shall respond within 2–3 seconds under normal load
  - Availability: System shall maintain 99.9% uptime
  - Security: All user data shall be encrypted using industry standards (AES-256 or equivalent)
  - Scalability: System shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users
  - Usability: Interface shall be mobile-friendly and easy to navigate
  - Reliability: Orders shall not be lost even during system failures

### 9. Assumptions
  - Users have access to smartphones or internet-enabled devices
  - Restaurants will maintain updated menu data
  - Payment gateways will be available and functional
  - Delivery drivers are available in service areas
  - GPS services are accessible for tracking

### 10. Constraints
  - Project must be completed within a 6-month timeline
  - Budget is limited to allocated funding
  - Must comply with local data protection regulations
  - System must support both Android and iOS platforms
  - Integration depends on third-party payment APIs

### 11. Risks
  - Payment gateway failures may disrupt transactions
  - High traffic may cause performance issues during peak hours
  - Restaurant delays may affect customer satisfaction
  - GPS inaccuracies may affect delivery tracking
  - Third-party API changes may break integrations

### 12. Dependencies
  - Payment gateway provider (e.g., Stripe, PayPal)
  - Cloud hosting provider (AWS / Azure / GCP)
  - Maps and GPS API (Google Maps API or equivalent)
  - SMS/Email notification service
  - Restaurant partner onboarding system
