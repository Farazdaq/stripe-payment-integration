# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

> AtPay 1.0.1 is a SaaS-based payment and subscription management system designed to help business managers and sales teams efficiently manage clients, packages, subscriptions, invoices, transactions, and financial analytics through an admin dashboard. It also provides a mobile application for clients to securely manage payments, track subscriptions, and access available packages. The system improves operational efficiency and supports financial decision-making through data insights.

## 1.4 Product Scope

##### 1.4.1 In Scope

1. Admin dashboard for business and sales management.
2. Client management (create, update, filter, and track clients).
3. Package and subscription management.
4. Invoice and transaction tracking.
5. Financial analytics and reporting dashboard.
6. Role-based access control for secure operations.
7. Mobile application for clients.
8. Secure online payments via Stripe integration.
9. Payment method management (cards and billing details).
10. Subscription tracking and status management.
11. Push notifications using Firebase.
12. Email notifications for critical system events

##### 1.4.1 Out Scope

1. Cash or offline payment handling.
2. Manual accounting system replacement
